Mate to mate

This board is blokes pointing other blokes to what’s on. It is not a doctor, a counsellor, or a crisis line.

Listings are checked before they go up, but times change. Ring the venue before you drive over.

If you or a mate is doing it tough, say something. Stay for a yarn. If it is heavier than that: Lifeline 13 11 14 · MensLine 1300 78 99 78 · 000 if someone is in danger.

You are welcome here whether you talk a lot or sit quiet with a cuppa.
